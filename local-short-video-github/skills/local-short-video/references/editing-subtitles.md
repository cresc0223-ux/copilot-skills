# Editing and Subtitle Standards

## Editing

- Keep source audio muted unless requested.
- Open on the clearest subject or action, not a random beginning frame.
- Mix wide/context, medium, and detail views when the material supports them.
- Store purposeful `source_start` values in the selected and used asset manifests.
- Use still images with subtle Ken Burns motion; avoid motion large enough to crop away the subject.
- Prefer direct cuts. Use transitions only when they support a change of section or time.
- Do not add music or sound effects by default.

## Subtitle styles

- `pop-yellow`: energetic product, retail, event, and social promotion.
- `clean-white`: neutral tutorial, service, interview, and corporate use.
- `cyan-tech`: technology, sport, and modern product use.
- `pink-lifestyle`: beauty, fashion, food, and lifestyle use when brand colors allow.
- `green-value`: sustainability, savings, home, and practical-use content.

Use a common installed font unless the project supplies a licensed font. Keep one or two lines, strong contrast, and a safe margin of at least 6% of frame width. Keep ordinary captions away from top and bottom platform controls.

Caption events must come from TTS timing. When one cue is too long, split its display into consecutive blocks inside the original time range without changing word order or content.

## Overlay distinction

Captions carry spoken text. Labels, prices, names, addresses, and calls to action are separate overlays and must not replace or alter spoken captions. Verify every overlay against user-provided facts and keep it inside the same safe area.
